W8 BEV picture test (Jev). Inactive. Run by hand only. Writes nothing.

WHY
Question: can a decision model sort what a picture shows into a closed list, the way the words box sorts a sentence into
a concern? Today the app takes no pictures and stores none. This is a lab bench to learn how it behaves, with test pictures only.

PATH
Run by hand -> Pick pictures (your list of public https links) -> Describe (a vision model, OFF) -> Build request
-> Jev (OFF) -> Tally (closed names only, counts, and "agree with your label" if you gave one).
The vision step turns a picture into a short factual description; Jev then answers one closed question about that text.
Nothing is sent anywhere until you switch a step on and add a credential in n8n yourself.

WHAT MAY BE USED
Your own photos and public pictures you may use. A person's face, a number plate or a street address must not be in a test picture.
Do NOT use pictures, charts or wording from the Zurich "2:1" deck: it is marked Restricted and covered by the NDA.
Only the idea may be reused, in our own words. Ask Christian Zeunert before any Zurich material goes through any outside service.

WHAT IT PROVES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
It measures agreement with the label you typed, on a handful of pictures. It says nothing about accuracy in the wild.
Nothing from this bench goes into the app unless Martin decides so after reading the tally.
